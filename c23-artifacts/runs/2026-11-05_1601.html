What this means for the audit

Controls 23 and 24 check presence, not volume. A PBI with one commit link passes exactly like a PBI with 174. The links were added because the evidence existed under the children and the parent was claiming none of it.

The 19 untouched items will keep failing 23/24 every week until the control is told what they are. They are test suites, user guides, research notes, support and POC work — deliverables that legitimately produce no repository artifact.

Board-wide, for context: 816 Done PBIs exist, 128 already carried both kinds, 443 could inherit (5,379 links), and 245 have no evidence anywhere below. Only the current cycle was written; the historical backlog was deliberately left alone because rewriting closed sprints bumps ChangedDate on hundreds of items and changes no past audit score.

Audit trail & reversal

  • Every modified item carries one comment: [Board Hygiene] Linked N commit(s) and M pull request link(s) inherited from the child item(s) #a, #b, … — written in the same revision as the links, so the item's history shows change and reason together.
  • Only /relations/- and System.History were touched. State, Iteration, Assigned To, Remaining Work and every other field are byte-identical to before.
  • Nothing was deleted. To reverse an item, remove the artifact links from its Relations tab; the comment stays as the record of what happened.
  • Run book: ado_inherit_artifacts.py — dry-run by default, --apply to write, --iters to scope by full iteration path. Plan JSON kept at ~/.ado_inherit_artifacts_plan.json.
